Quick answers

Frequently asked.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 2 pm are delivered the same day across Portland metro. Outside metro, next‑day delivery is standard.

Is everything really organic?

Every product carrying the GreenCrate seal is USDA‑organic certified or sourced directly from verified small‑scale farms we audit twice a year.

Can I pause my subscription?

Absolutely. Skip a week, pause indefinitely, or cancel — all in one click, no questions asked, no fees.

Do you ship nationwide?

Currently we deliver to Oregon and Washington. Nationwide expansion is on the way — join our newsletter to be notified first.
